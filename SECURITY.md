# Reporting a security issue

Please report security-sensitive issues privately to **contact@strilk.com**. Include the repository, affected revision or version, a concise description of the issue, its expected impact and any relevant redacted diagnostic evidence.

Use a public issue for ordinary bugs that do not expose sensitive security details. Avoid including credentials, personal information or confidential third-party material in either channel.

Research must remain within the applicable authorization and project rules. STRILK will review reports and coordinate any remediation and publication with the relevant parties. This policy does not promise a bounty, a response deadline, or authorization to test systems outside the scope described in [our laboratory policy](https://strilk.com/laboratoire).

Project-specific SECURITY.md files take precedence where they provide a more specific reporting route.
